Pierre-Yves Pointereau

Senior software engineer, front-end · UI architect · Sydney

I build the foundations product teams ship on: component libraries, accessibility practice, and fast, stable pages.

At Humanitix I created and look after our Svelte component library, led the move to Svelte 5, and built our automated accessibility testing against WCAG 2.2. Before that I spent seven years as Front End Architect at News Corp Australia, working on the metro news network and its multi-brand design system.

Most of my work now runs through coding agents. I’m most interested in what makes their output trustworthy: review and QA agents, verification pipelines, and a person signing off where it matters.
