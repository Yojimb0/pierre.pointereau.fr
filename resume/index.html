Summary

Senior front-end engineer and UI architect with 20 years of experience building large consumer web products, from News Corp Australia’s network of around 15 news sites to Humanitix’s ticketing and checkout platform. I build the foundations other engineers ship on: component libraries and design systems, accessibility practice and testing, and fast, stable pages. Since 2026 most of my delivery runs through coding agents, and I focus on making their output trustworthy: agent skills for code review and cross-browser QA, and verification pipelines with human sign-off built in. Outside work, I use agents to build and ship full-stack products end to end, including DailyPirate, a live daily puzzle game.
